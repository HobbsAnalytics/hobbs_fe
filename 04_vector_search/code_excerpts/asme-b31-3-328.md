title: "Welder and procedure qualification"
source: "ASME B31.3"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: weld_joint_spec_mismatch

Welds must be made to a qualified welding procedure by welders who are qualified for that procedure, and the drawing should reference the procedure. A weld made with an unqualified procedure is not acceptable regardless of how it looks. A reviewer checks that the weld details cite a procedure number and that it suits the base materials and thickness.
