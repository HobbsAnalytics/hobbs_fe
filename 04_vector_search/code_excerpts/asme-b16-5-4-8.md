title: "Bolting and gasket selection for flanged joints"
source: "ASME B16.5"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: none
---
Flanged joints need bolting and a gasket matched to the flange class, facing and service. A gasket that is too soft for a high-class joint, or bolts of the wrong grade, can leak even when the flange class is correct. A reviewer confirms that the bill of materials lists bolting and gaskets compatible with the flange class and temperature.
