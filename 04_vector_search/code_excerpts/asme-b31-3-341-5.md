title: "Records of weld examination"
source: "ASME B31.3"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: weld_joint_spec_mismatch
---
Examination results must be recorded and kept, identifying the weld, the method, the date, the examiner and the result. These records show that each weld was actually examined to the required extent. A reviewer checks that weld numbers on the drawing are unique so each can be traced to a record.
