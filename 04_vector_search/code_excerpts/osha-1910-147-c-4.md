title: "Written energy control procedures for each machine"
source: "OSHA 29 CFR 1910"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: missing_loto_tag
---
The employer must document a procedure for each machine that states the scope, purpose, steps for shutting down and isolating it, the steps for placing and removing locks and tags, and how to test that the isolation is effective. A reviewer checks that each piece of equipment on the drawing is tied to a procedure number or isolation list.
