title: "No block valve between the system and its relief device"
source: "ASME B31.3"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: missing_pressure_relief_notation
---
A valve placed between the protected system and its relief device could close off the protection. Where an isolation valve is allowed for maintenance, it must be locked or sealed open and controlled by procedure. A reviewer checks the drawing for any valve beside a relief device and for a lock-open or car-seal note.
