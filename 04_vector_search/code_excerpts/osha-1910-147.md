title: "Control of hazardous energy: lockout and tagout"

is_paraphrase: true

Before anyone services or maintains a machine or piece of equipment, the equipment must be isolated from all sources of hazardous energy and locked or tagged out so that it cannot start up or release stored energy. Each machine needs identified energy isolation points, such as breakers, valves and disconnects, where a lock or tag can be applied. A reviewer checks the drawing for lockout and tagout isolation points, tags or notes on each piece of equipment, and flags equipment that shows no lockout or isolation provision.
