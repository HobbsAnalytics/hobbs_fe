A relief device must be big enough to pass the largest flow that could over-pressure the system without letting pressure climb above the allowed limit. Sizing is based on the worst credible scenario, such as a blocked outlet or fire. A reviewer checks that a capacity or orifice size is shown and that it is supported by a sizing calculation reference.
