title: "Release from lockout: checks before restoring energy"
source: "OSHA 29 CFR 1910"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: missing_loto_tag
---
Before locks are removed and energy is restored, the area must be checked for tools and people, the equipment must be intact, and each lock must be removed by the person who applied it. A reviewer checks that the procedure for the equipment includes a restart step.
