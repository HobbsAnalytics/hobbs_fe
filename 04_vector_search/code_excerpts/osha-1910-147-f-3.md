title: "Group lockout and shift changes"
source: "OSHA 29 CFR 1910"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: missing_loto_tag
---
When a group of workers or more than one shift works on the same equipment, each person must be able to apply a personal lock, and the transfer of control between shifts must keep protection continuous. A reviewer checks that multi-lock hasps or group lockout provisions are indicated on larger equipment.
