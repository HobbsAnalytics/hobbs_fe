title: "Periodic inspection of energy control procedures"

is_paraphrase: true

The employer must inspect each energy control procedure at least once a year to confirm it is being followed and is still adequate. The inspection is done by an authorized employee other than the one using the procedure. A reviewer checks for inspection records or a review date on the procedure list.
