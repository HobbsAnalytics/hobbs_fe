title: "Sequence for applying lockout: shut down, isolate, lock, release stored energy, verify"

is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: missing_loto_tag
---
The application of controls follows a set order: prepare by understanding the energy sources, shut the equipment down in the normal way, operate the isolating devices, apply the locks or tags, release or restrain any stored energy, and verify the isolation by trying to start the equipment. A reviewer checks that the drawing shows the isolating device for every energy source, including electrical, pressure and gravity.
