title: "Egress paths must be kept clear of obstructions"
source: "IBC"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: egress_width_under_code
---
The width of an egress path must not be reduced by equipment, storage or fixtures beyond the small projections allowed. A reviewer checks that equipment on the drawing, such as panels, skids and piping, does not encroach on the clear width of the path.
