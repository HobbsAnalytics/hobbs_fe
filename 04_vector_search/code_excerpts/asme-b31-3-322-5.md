title: "Thermal relief for blocked-in liquid lines"

is_paraphrase: true

A liquid line that can be isolated between two valves can build very high pressure if the trapped liquid warms and expands. A small thermal relief valve is usually provided on such sections. A reviewer looks for sections between two closed valves, such as cold LNG lines, and checks that thermal relief is shown.
