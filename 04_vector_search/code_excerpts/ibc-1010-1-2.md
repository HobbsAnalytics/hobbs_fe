title: "Direction of door swing in egress"
source: "IBC"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: egress_width_under_code
---
Doors serving higher occupant loads or hazardous spaces must swing in the direction of egress travel. A door that opens against the flow can jam in a crowd. A reviewer checks the swing arrow on the drawing for these spaces.
