title: "Post-weld heat treatment"
source: "ASME B31.3"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: weld_joint_spec_mismatch
---
Some materials and thicknesses need heat treatment after welding to relieve stress and restore toughness. The drawing or the weld procedure must state when it is required. A reviewer checks that thick-wall or alloy welds shown on the drawing carry the heat-treatment note when the material and thickness call for it.
