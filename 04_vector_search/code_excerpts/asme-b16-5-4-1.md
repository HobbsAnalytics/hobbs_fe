Flanges are grouped into pressure classes, and the class number is a label, not a pressure in psi. The real allowable pressure for a class depends on the material and on the operating temperature. A reviewer treats the class on the drawing as a claim to be checked against the line's service conditions, never as proof that the flange is adequate.
