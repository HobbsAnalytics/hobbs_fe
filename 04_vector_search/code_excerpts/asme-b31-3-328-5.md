title: "Weld joint types and preparation"

is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: weld_joint_spec_mismatch

The joint type, such as butt weld, socket weld or branch connection, and its preparation must suit the pipe size, thickness and service. Some joint types are not permitted in severe services. A reviewer checks that the joint type shown on the drawing is permitted for the fluid service and that the weld size or bevel is called out.
