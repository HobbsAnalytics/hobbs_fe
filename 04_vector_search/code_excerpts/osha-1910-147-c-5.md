title: "Lockout and tagout devices must be durable and identifiable"
source: "OSHA 29 CFR 1910"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: missing_loto_tag
---
Locks and tags must be provided by the employer, must withstand the conditions where they are used, must be standardized by color, shape or size, and must identify the person applying them. A lock is the preferred method because it physically holds the isolating device. A reviewer checks that the drawing shows a lockable isolating device, such as a lockable breaker or valve, not just a note.
