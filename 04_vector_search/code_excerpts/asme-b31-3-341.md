title: "Weld examination and joint requirements"

Welds in process piping must be examined to a defined extent, and the examination method and amount depend on the fluid service and the weld type. Higher-risk services call for more radiography or other volumetric examination, and a drawing must show the examination requirement for each weld or joint. A reviewer checks that the weld symbol, joint type and examination percentage on the drawing meet the requirement for the line's service, and flags a weld whose examination is less than required.
