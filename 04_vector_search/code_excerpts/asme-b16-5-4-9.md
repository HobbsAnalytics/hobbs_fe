title: "Pressure testing limits for flanges"
source: "ASME B16.5"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: flange_rating_mismatch
---
Flanged joints must withstand a pressure test, and the test pressure is limited by the lowest rated component in the system. A flange class that is adequate for operation may still restrict the test pressure the line can take. A reviewer checks that the lowest-rated flange in the test boundary is high enough for the planned test.
