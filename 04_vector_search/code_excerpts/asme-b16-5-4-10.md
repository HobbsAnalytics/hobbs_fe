title: "Class selection must not be inferred from pipe schedule or size"
source: "ASME B16.5"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: flange_rating_mismatch
---
A flange class cannot be assumed from the pipe wall thickness, schedule or nominal size. Thick-walled pipe can still be fitted with a low-class flange that is the real limit on the line. A reviewer confirms that a flange class is explicitly stated for each flanged connection and checks that class against the design conditions.
