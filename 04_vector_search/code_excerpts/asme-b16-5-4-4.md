title: "Allowable pressure drops at higher temperature"
source: "ASME B16.5"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: flange_rating_mismatch
---
As metal temperature rises its strength falls, so the allowable pressure of a flange class falls with it. A flange that is fine at ambient temperature may be undersized for a hot service line. A reviewer checks the design temperature, not just the operating pressure, and flags any flange whose class only works at a lower temperature than the line sees.
