title: "Extent of required examination by fluid service"
source: "ASME B31.3"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: weld_joint_spec_mismatch
---
Normal fluid service needs a basic visual examination plus a percentage of random radiography. Severe cyclic or high-pressure services, and categories with a higher consequence of leakage, need a larger share or all welds examined. A reviewer finds the fluid service category for the line and confirms that the examination percentage shown on the drawing is at least what that category requires.
