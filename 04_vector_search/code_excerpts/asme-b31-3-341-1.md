The owner is responsible for making sure the required examination is carried out, and the inspector must be independent of the contractor doing the work. Examination is a quality-control step in addition to the fabricator's own checks. A reviewer looks for the examination plan or notes on the drawing and checks that someone other than the welder is responsible for it.
