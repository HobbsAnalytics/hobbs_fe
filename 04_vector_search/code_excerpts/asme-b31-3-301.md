title: "Design conditions for piping"
source: "ASME B31.3"

related_flaw_type: none
---
The design pressure, design temperature and loads must be set for each piping system and they must cover the most severe coincident conditions in service. All later checks, from wall thickness to flange class, depend on these values. A reviewer confirms that every line has design pressure and temperature stated on the line list or drawing.
