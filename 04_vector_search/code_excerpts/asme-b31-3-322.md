Piping and equipment that could see pressure above its design limit must be protected by a pressure-relief device, and the drawing must show it. Typical causes are a blocked outlet, thermal expansion of trapped liquid, or a control valve failing open. A reviewer checks the P and ID or drawing for a relief valve or rupture disc, its set pressure and its tag on every system that can be over-pressured, and flags a system that shows no relief protection.
