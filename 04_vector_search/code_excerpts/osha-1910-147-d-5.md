title: "Stored or residual energy must be relieved"
source: "OSHA 29 CFR 1910"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: missing_loto_tag
---
After the isolating devices are locked, any stored energy such as pressure, springs, elevated parts or charged capacitors must be relieved, disconnected or restrained. A reviewer checks that bleed, vent or drain points are shown where a pressurized system can retain energy after isolation.
