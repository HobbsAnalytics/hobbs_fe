title: "Leak testing of completed piping"
source: "ASME B31.3"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: none
---
Completed piping is leak tested before it goes into service, normally with water at a pressure above the design pressure, or with a gas test where water cannot be used. The test pressure depends on the design pressure and the allowable stress at test temperature. A reviewer checks that the test pressure and medium are stated and that no component in the test boundary is rated below the test pressure.
