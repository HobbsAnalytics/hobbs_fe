title: "Exit signs and illuminated exit marking"
source: "IBC"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: egress_width_under_code
---
Exits and exit access doors must be marked by approved, illuminated exit signs that are visible from the egress path. A reviewer checks that exit signs are shown at every exit and at changes of direction in the path.
