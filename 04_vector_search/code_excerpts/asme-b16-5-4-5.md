title: "Mixed classes at a flanged joint and at equipment connections"
source: "ASME B16.5"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: flange_rating_mismatch
---
Mating flanges at a joint should have the same class and facing, and a flange on a nozzle must match the nozzle it bolts to. When a line of one class connects to equipment of another class, the weaker side limits the joint. A reviewer checks both sides of every flanged connection on the drawing and flags a mismatch that leaves the weaker flange under-rated.
