title: "Materials and service temperature limits"
source: "ASME B31.3"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: none
---
Materials must be suitable for the fluid and for the lowest and highest temperatures in service. Cold services such as liquefied natural gas need materials that stay tough at low temperature. A reviewer checks that the material shown on the drawing is permitted for the service temperature range.
