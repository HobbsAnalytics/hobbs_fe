title: "Flange pressure class must suit design pressure and temperature"
source: "ASME B16.5"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: flange_rating_mismatch
---
Every flange on a line must carry a pressure class (150, 300, 600, 900, 1500 or 2500) whose allowable pressure at the line's design temperature is at least the line's design pressure. A flange rating is not a single number: it falls as temperature rises, and it depends on the material group. A reviewer reads the design pressure and temperature from the line list or P&ID, looks up the allowable pressure for the flange's class and material at that temperature, and confirms the drawn class is high enough. A class that is too low for the line rating, for example a Class 150 flange on a 300 psig line, is a failure.
