title: "Corridor width"
source: "IBC"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: egress_width_under_code
---
Corridors that serve as egress must meet a minimum width, which is larger for corridors serving a bigger occupant load. A reviewer compares the corridor width on the drawing to the minimum and to the occupant load of the spaces it serves.
