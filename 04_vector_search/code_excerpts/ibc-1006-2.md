title: "Number of exits and exit access doorways"
source: "IBC"
is_paraphrase: true
related_flaw_type: egress_width_under_code
---
Spaces must have enough exits or exit access doorways for their occupant load and for the travel distance. More occupants, or a more hazardous use, require more separate exits. A reviewer counts the exits shown for each space and checks that the number meets the occupant load requirement.
